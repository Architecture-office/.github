# Architecture Office @ ELEKS

The **Architecture Office** is the center of excellence for software architecture at [ELEKS](https://eleks.com) - a global software engineering and technology consulting company.

We bring together ELEKS architects across projects and domains to set architecture standards, grow architectural talent, and ensure our clients get technology decisions that serve their business goals.

## What we do

- **Architecture standards & governance** - reference architectures, decision-making frameworks (ADRs), design review practices, and quality gates applied across client engagements.
- **Career development for architects** - a structured career ladder based on SFIA 9, from Associate Architect to Strategic Architect (Mini-CTO), with competency frameworks, assessment criteria, and promotion paths.
- **Knowledge sharing** - architecture playbooks, patterns, templates, and lessons learned from real-world delivery.
- **Client advisory** - supporting engagements where architects act as trusted technology advisors, aligning technical strategy with business outcomes.

## Architect career ladder

| Role                           | SFIA level | Focus                                                          |
|--------------------------------|------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Associate Architect            | 4          | Component & interface design within a team                     |
| Application Architect          | 5          | Application-level architecture and authoritative guidance      |
| Solution Architect             | 6          | Cross-system solutions, high-level decisions, shaping policies |
| Strategic Architect (Mini-CTO) | 6-7        | Client technology strategy as part of business strategy        |

The defining trait of the ladder: as architects grow, the business dimension of their decisions grows with the technical one - from "technically correct" to "best business outcome given technical capabilities."
